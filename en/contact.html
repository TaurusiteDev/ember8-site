Frequently asked questions
Is a first session non-committal?

A first session is not a long-term commitment. It is an introduction — to yourself and to the way of working.

Do you offer payment in instalments?

For the deepening programme, payment in two instalments is possible. Get in touch to discuss the options.

Do you work with insurance?

My sessions are currently outside regular insurance coverage. Some supplementary insurance plans partially reimburse coaching — check with your insurer.

Can I also do this online?

Yes, coaching sessions can be done online. Trauma release and breathwork are only offered in person, since physical presence is essential for safe, careful guidance.

Ready for a first step?

You don't need to be certain of anything yet. A message is enough for us to look at what fits.
