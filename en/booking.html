Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

Do I need to know what my 'problem' is?

No. You don't need to know exactly what your 'problem' is. Many people arrive without a clear question. That's completely fine. Clarity about what needs attention often emerges naturally during the session.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation up to 48 hours before the session: free of charge. After that, 50% of the rate will be charged.

Is this guidance suitable for serious trauma?

I work in a trauma-conscious and trauma-informed way. Trauma release can also be supportive for complex PTSD, provided this is appropriate for your situation and any ongoing treatment. That's why we always carefully assess whether this guidance is safe and responsible at this time. If needed, I coordinate with your treating professional or advise additional specialised care.

Do you also work with organisations?

Yes. Alongside individual sessions, Ember8 offers tailored guidance for teams and organisations — coaching, team guidance and group sessions. Get in touch to discuss the options.

Can my employer cover the cost of this guidance?

Yes. Many employers invest in the health and sustainable employability of their staff. Depending on your situation, guidance through Ember8 may be covered by a vitality budget, a training budget, or as part of a reintegration or recovery programme. Options vary by employer. I'm happy to think it through with you.
