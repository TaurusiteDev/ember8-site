Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

Do I need to know what my 'problem' is?

No. Many people arrive without a clear question. That's fine — the session finds its own direction.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation up to 48 hours before the session: free of charge. After that, 50% of the rate will be charged.

Is this suitable for serious trauma?

I work in a trauma-informed way, but I am not a psychotherapist. For complex PTSD I refer to qualified therapists.
