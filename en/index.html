About me

For years I looked at others. Until my body forced me to look at myself.

27 years in the police force taught me to look at others. The years after that finally taught me to look at myself.

When I became a whistleblower in 2019, my body showed me what I had ignored for years. That was the start of a search.

For seven years I explored old wisdom, body-oriented work, and the patterns that keep steering us. The turning point came while climbing the highest mountain in North Africa. That's where my slogan Break the Loop was born.

Today I guide people who want to explore what their body has been trying to tell them for a long time. Not to become someone else, but to live more freely.
