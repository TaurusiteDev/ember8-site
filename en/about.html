About Ember8

Ember8 was born from the conviction that your body isn't working against you — it's trying to protect you.

For a long time I looked for change mostly in my head. Until I discovered that my body was telling a story words couldn't reach.

That discovery changed not only my own life, but today forms the foundation of Ember8.

My mission is to guide you toward more calm, resilience, and connection with yourself.

Our approach

Lasting change doesn't begin with control, but with safety.

1. Listening

We don't start with the story, but with what your body shows. By truly listening to your body, your signals and your nervous system, clarity emerges.

2. Regulating

With BRTT®, TRB® and body-oriented guidance, your nervous system gets room to release built-up tension. Your body is allowed to drop out of survival mode and come back into balance.

3. Integrating

What surfaces gets attention, so change isn't only felt during a session but also carries into daily life. This is how you make new choices that truly fit you.

When is this guidance not suitable?

Your safety always comes first with us. That's why this guidance is not currently suitable, or only possible after consulting a doctor or treating professional, in the following situations.

Acute psychosis

This guidance is not suitable during an acute psychosis. We advise treatment and stabilisation under the care of a doctor or psychiatrist first.

Untreated epilepsy

Untreated epilepsy can make this guidance risky. Please consult your treating doctor first.

Severe dissociative symptoms

Careful assessment is important with severe dissociative symptoms. Discuss with your treating professional beforehand whether this guidance is a good fit right now.

Recent or unstable heart problems

Have you recently had heart problems or are you experiencing unstable heart symptoms? Please consult your GP or cardiologist first.

Pregnancy

Depending on the stage of pregnancy and your medical situation, this guidance may be adapted or postponed. Please get in touch with us beforehand so we can look together at what's responsible.

Not sure?

Not sure whether this guidance is right for you? Feel free to get in touch. We're happy to think it through with you and look together at what a safe and suitable next step would be.
